This policy describes the current Android version of Sector Rebound, a local-first top-down ricochet arcade game. The developer or publisher is [[PUBLISHER_NAME_REQUIRED]]. For privacy inquiries, contact [[PUBLIC_CONTACT_REQUIRED]].

Information the game uses

Local game data. The game stores your preferences, selected modes, Custom Match drafts and presets, Solo match records and statistics, and earned or equipped paint finishes and insignia in the app's local WebView storage. These records support gameplay, personalization and the on-device Dossier. Solo play does not require an account.

Nearby Wi-Fi play. When you choose to host or join a local multiplayer session, the devices use Wi-Fi discovery and direct local TCP communication. The two connected devices exchange player inputs, match rules and match-state information. The guest can receive the host's local IP address and port to connect. These exchanges are with the other nearby player's device, not with a developer-operated matchmaking service. A peer's IP address is visible on the same local network where they play.

User-controlled backup. You can generate a text backup of eligible game data in the Dossier. The game shows this backup to you for copying or sharing at your discretion; the current version does not automatically upload it to the developer. The way a third-party service handles copies you choose to share is governed by its own practices.

Developer-operated collection and services

The reviewed version does not include developer-run accounts, remote matchmaking, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or connected purchases. It does not send your Dossier statistics or exported backup to a developer-run server. This description does not cover unrelated processing by your Android device, app store, network provider or an external service you choose to use.

How information is used or shared

Local records are used to provide Solo gameplay, preferences, unlocks and history. During a local multiplayer session, the two participating devices share the connection and gameplay information needed for that session. If you choose to copy or share a backup, the recipients and services you select may receive its contents. The current version does not sell player information.

Security

Game pages are bundled inside the Android application, and the WebView is configured to block remote network loading from those pages. Local Wi-Fi play uses direct TCP communication; it is not represented as end-to-end encrypted. Avoid connecting on untrusted Wi-Fi networks. Keep copies of your backup text private if they contain information you do not wish to share.

Retention and deletion

Local records stay on your device while the app and its data remain installed. You can reset some choices in the app; to remove all app-local records, clear the Android app's storage or uninstall the app. Copies you voluntarily export are separate and must be deleted wherever you stored or shared them. There is no developer-hosted player-account database to delete in the reviewed version.

Changes

If future updates introduce online accounts, analytics, advertising, payments, cloud progress, remote servers or materially different data handling, this policy will be revised before those features are released.
