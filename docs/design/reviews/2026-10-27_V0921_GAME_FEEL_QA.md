## Changes made in actual running game
1. **Menu cleanup.** Removed the build ID, redundant no-account statement, long exposition and duplicate ready status. Keeps Sector Rebound logo, live tank showcase, Quick Play, Play Together, Match Settings and How To Play. No fictitious profile or premium store buttons.
2. **Live showcase.** The home arena canvas draws the **real in-game tank and mech renderers**, switching every 8 seconds and using restrained slow rotation, treads and mech stance rather than an unrelated animated render. The title changes honestly from ARMORED RESPONSE to MECHANIZED RESPONSE. OS reduced-motion preference holds the tank still.
3. **Pilot readability experiment.** Replaced the little mechanical/drone-looking pilot with a straightforward **human top-down silhouette**: two independently walking boots, narrow torso, two restrained arms, distinct front helmet/visor and a deliberately tiny pistol. Same hitbox and combat attributes. Requires real phone evaluation as zooming out also reduces on-screen pilot size.
4. **Measured zoom trial.** Camera targets changed from fallback/tank/mech/foot `1.24/1.22/1.16/1.32` to `1.15/1.13/1.08/1.22` (around **7% more visible field width/height**). Existing zoom floor, easing, variable map bounds, HUD and unit class distinction remain. Exact real-game pullback depends on device and map-size floor.
5. **Music proof.** New original lightweight procedural **WebAudio** synthesized pulses: quiet 96-BPM menu and restrained 116-BPM arena scene from a small authored note palette. Starts only after user touch; no music asset, generation credit, streaming, ads, external library or music permissions. Settings now have independent **SOUND FX** and **MUSIC** toggles; music can be muted with no effect on SFX. Keep music below gunfire/hits. Do not call it finished soundtrack until owner listens on physical Android speakers.
