**Full description:**
Every sector is different. Every shot is a decision.

Sector Rebound is a top-down ricochet combat game where sharp angles, clever positioning and precise timing matter more than charging straight ahead.

Pilot tanks and other armored vehicles through procedurally generated mazes. Bank projectiles around corners, outmaneuver your opponents, and when your vehicle's armor gives out, keep fighting on foot.

Choose Survival, pilot-only Close Quarters, scored Free-for-All or Team Deathmatch. Set up Custom Matches with your own rules, or use Quick Play to rotate through selected modes and fresh mazes.

Earn and equip offline paint finishes and pilot insignia, and track Solo progress in your on-device Dossier. No account is required for Solo play.

Nearby Local Wi-Fi multiplayer is experimental and still awaits final physical two-phone acceptance; do not market it as production-ready before that gate is passed.
