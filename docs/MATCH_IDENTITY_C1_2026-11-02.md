# Sector Rebound — Phase C1: authoritative match colors and randomized Solo teams
**2026-11-02** · [Experimental branch](https://github.com/PlayerEden/Ricochet-Rumble/tree/experiment/match-identity-colors-c1) based on the fully CI-verified v0.9.69 B2 branch. **No merge to stable `main` or owner APK yet.**

## What changed
- **FFA**: each individual actor gets one unique color from the six-color tested FIELDGRID palette, shuffled by the sector seed using Fisher–Yates. A player isn't permanently Cyan; colors remain fixed for the match (including death/respawn) and are included in the host-authoritative config packet so the guest observes **the same physical colors**. No completely random RGB, confusing near-identical allies or cosmetic/paint ambiguity; six seats maximum and six palette entries.
- **Real teams**: each match now generates explicit `config.teamIds` (and `teamIdsMode` for legacy/mutated-mode safety); the same IDs determine `teamOf()`, bot targeting, friendly fire, physical collision, Forward Bay deployment, vehicle/actor coloring, scored TDM team IDs and result ownership. This is **not** just a superficial local recolor.
- **Solo Quick Play** team rounds choose a side at the first encounter and alternate in successive team rounds, even with FFA matches between them. Team survival, scored TDM and their AI allies/opponents follow the real new side. **Explicit menu scored TDM and LAN host vs guest** remain canonical Cyan/Coral for backwards compatibility until Stage D host/guest squad rosters.
- **Custom Match** team modes have a new accessible **YOUR TEAM** dropdown with RANDOM (default), CYAN and CORAL; FFA modes disable this selector. One chosen match config freezes the assignments; rematches regenerate for RANDOM while locked sides remain fixed. Existing version-1 presets, absent field and old preferences safely normalize.
- **CTF and HUD**: true local CTF side selects the appropriate home/enemy flag and dynamic text, team score labels identify which side belongs to YOU, and team spawns face inward regardless of seat index. The host and guest use consistent color names (no per-screen swap). FFA participant labels use the actual color identity for each match.
- **Network**: state snapshots already replicate `state.config`; now the same packet carries `teamIds`, `teamIdsMode` and `ffaColors`, with strict validation (correct length, team domain, unique palette values for FFA). Legacy snapshots without the new fields fall back to classic parity and fixed palette; no new client-authoritative hit/damage/KO requests.

## Scope and safety
Preserve A1–A3 real bullet trajectories/AI physical turn/firing, B1 Friendly Fire ON/OFF and score-neutral team kills, and B2 visible ally traces/guest-only intent metadata. Hangar cosmetics cannot determine hitboxes or teams. No new matchmaking, online account, paid cosmetics, sound, camera or control changes. Stable `main` and the signed v0.9.69 APK remain the rollback.
**Not implemented in C1**: configurable host/guest team swap and individual bot side rosters (Stage D); formal faction lore/insignia; full manual two-phone LAN validation.
