## New room behavior
- In local Wi-Fi room with no connected guest, **FFA** now offers real host vs **1–5 AI opponents** with distinct FFA identity; configured additional bots 0–4 plus actual reserved slot 1 AI. No empty no-enemy match. Match may end by ordinary pilot survival.
- **Team Survival** now offers 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 real Cyan/Coral sides with 0/2/4 additional bots; host-aligned bot teammates are physically allied, the host chooses faction. Both Team Survival and scored TDM now normalize uneven extra bot counts to balanced pairs, including lobby rule changes.
- **Co-op vs Bots** now offers host and real AI ally at reserved slot 1, facing 1–4 opposing AI; Co-op with zero opposing AI is invalid and cannot start. The AI ally is never falsely labeled as a human.
- Bot dropdown and room summary display true numbers, human/AI distinction and mode roster; host-only bot difficulty is visible even with zero *additional* bots.
- Host-only **non-scored** HUD now shows living opposing actors, not arbitrary reserved seat 1 lives or allied bots. Score remains the real scored TDM ledger for TDM only.
- Match over → NEW SECTOR or CHANGE RULES; same live local host socket across rematches, no Dossier/paid progression for LAN bots. Existing two-human guest Ready, teams and D2 series remain fully regression-gated.
- **Not here:** mid-match spectator, queue/promotion, native multi-accept, remote internet play or physical two-phone acceptance.
