## What changed (and did not)
A bot occasionally earns a modest faster direct vehicle shot (by reducing visible aim-acquisition delay) after its first two completed shots. The pattern is staggered by map seed/actor rather than true randomness or tracking whether the player is winning. Approximate eligible shot frequency: **Relaxed 1 in 7; Standard 1 in 5; Challenge 1 in 4**. Fewer may qualify if moving too fast, off-angle, facing an unseen target, planning a bank, or targeting a vulnerable foot pilot. Quick timing never bypasses M2-AI1 real physical turn/low-speed requirement, final .095-radian muzzle aim, LOS, team FF safety, projectile trajectory, ammo capacity or cooldown. On-screen muzzle charge aligns with actual shorter delay. Player controls, approved art, scoring, saves and LAN host rules unchanged.
